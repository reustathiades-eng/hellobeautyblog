## Welcome to Hello Beauty

Thank you for visiting **Hello Beauty**, founded in San Francisco in 2009 by Sonja Shin, a health journalist, model, certified life coach and meditation teacher with thousands of hours of training around the U.S.

Shin specializes in working with executives in high-stress industries, using tools like mindfulness, meditation, positive psychology and visualization for stress relief and to rewire the brain.

### About Sonja Shin

Shin has decades of experience reporting, producing and researching news at CBS and NBC stations, and for "The Today Show" and "NBC Nightly News" in NYC. She has been a science writer for Northwestern, Stanford and UCSF, and handled public affairs and development for NASA in Silicon Valley.

As a Korean-American daughter of an immigrant, Shin is passionate about sharing information that empowers people of all walks of life.

### Our Mission

After hundreds of thousands of visits, Hello Beauty has evolved into a **mindful beauty and self-care site**. We hope you find information here that empowers you on your journey.

*Beauty is in the eye of the beholder. Do you see the beauty in you – inside and out?*
