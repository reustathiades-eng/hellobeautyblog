## About Boss Alive

Boss Alive is a celebration of the modern, multifaceted woman. Launched in 2020, this Eau de Parfum embodies a sense of purpose and authenticity that defines today's woman.

### The Inspiration

Created by master perfumer Annick Ménardo, Boss Alive captures the spirit of a woman who pursues her passions with confidence and conviction. The fragrance tells a story of empowerment and self-expression.

### The Composition

The opening bursts with a fresh, fruity accord of crisp apple and juicy plum, softened by the sweetness of blackcurrant. This vibrant beginning gives way to a sophisticated heart where jasmine sambac takes center stage, enhanced by aromatic thyme and delicate olive blossom.

The base reveals a sensual, grounding accord of creamy sandalwood, warm cedar, and a whisper of vanilla that leaves a memorable trail.

### Who Is It For?

Boss Alive is perfect for the woman who:
- Values authenticity and self-expression
- Leads an active, purposeful life
- Appreciates sophisticated yet approachable fragrances
- Wants a versatile scent for day-to-night wear

### Our Verdict

A beautifully balanced fragrance that manages to be both fresh and sophisticated. The jasmine sambac heart gives it a distinctive character, while the woody base ensures longevity without being overpowering. An excellent choice for the modern professional woman.
