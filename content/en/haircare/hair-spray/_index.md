faq:
  - question: "What's the difference between light, medium, and strong hold hair sprays?"
    answer: "Light hold sprays provide flexible movement and natural feel, perfect for everyday styling. Medium hold offers more control while maintaining some flexibility, ideal for most hairstyles. Strong hold provides maximum staying power for elaborate styles, updos, and special occasions where your hair needs to stay perfectly in place all day."
  - question: "How do I choose the right hair spray for my hair type?"
    answer: "Fine hair benefits from lightweight, volumizing formulas that won't weigh hair down. Thick or coarse hair can handle stronger formulas with more hold. Curly hair works well with humidity-resistant sprays that define curls without causing frizz. Color-treated hair should use UV-protective formulas to prevent fading."
  - question: "Can hair spray damage my hair with regular use?"
    answer: "Quality hair sprays with nourishing ingredients are safe for daily use when applied properly. Look for alcohol-free or low-alcohol formulas, and always brush out product before washing. Regular shampooing removes buildup, and using heat protectant sprays can actually help shield hair from styling damage."
  - question: "What's the proper way to apply hair spray for best results?"
    answer: "Hold the bottle 8-12 inches away from your hair and spray in short bursts rather than continuous streams. Work in sections, spraying each area lightly and building coverage as needed. For volume, spray at the roots while lifting sections. For hold, spray the finished style from multiple angles for even coverage."
