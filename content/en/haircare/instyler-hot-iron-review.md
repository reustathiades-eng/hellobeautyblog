The InStyler Hot Iron is a revolutionary styling tool that combines the benefits of a curling iron and flat iron. Learn how to use this innovative device for salon-worthy results.

## How to Use an InStyler

The InStyler works differently than traditional styling tools. Its rotating barrel and ceramic plates work together to smooth, polish, and add volume to your hair.
