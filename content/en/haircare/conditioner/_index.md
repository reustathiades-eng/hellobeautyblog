    answer: "Regular conditioners are designed for daily use and provide basic moisture and detangling. Deep conditioners contain higher concentrations of nourishing ingredients and are used weekly or bi-weekly for intensive repair and hydration."
  - question: "Should I apply conditioner to my scalp?"
    answer: "Generally, conditioner should be applied from mid-length to ends of your hair, avoiding the scalp. Applying conditioner to the scalp can weigh down hair and potentially clog pores, especially for those with oily hair."
