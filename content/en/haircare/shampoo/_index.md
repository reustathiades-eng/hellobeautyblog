  - question: "What's the difference between sulfate-free and regular shampoo?"
    answer: "Sulfate-free shampoos use gentler cleansing agents that don't strip natural oils as harshly. They're ideal for color-treated, dry, or chemically-processed hair, though they may not lather as much as traditional sulfate shampoos."
  - question: "Can I use the same shampoo year-round?"
