  - question: "How often should I use a hair mask?"
    answer: "For most hair types, using a hair mask once or twice a week is ideal. If your hair is severely damaged, dry, or chemically treated, you may benefit from using a mask 2-3 times per week initially, then reducing frequency as your hair condition improves."
  - question: "What's the difference between a hair mask and regular conditioner?"
    answer: "Hair masks contain higher concentrations of nourishing ingredients and are designed to be left on longer than regular conditioners. They penetrate deeper into the hair shaft to provide intensive repair and hydration, while conditioners primarily work on the hair's surface for daily maintenance."
  - question: "Can I leave a hair mask on overnight?"
    answer: "While some masks are formulated for overnight use, most should only be left on for 10-20 minutes as directed. Leaving protein-rich masks on too long can cause protein overload, making hair brittle. Always follow the specific instructions on your chosen hair mask product."
  - question: "Should I apply hair mask to wet or dry hair?"
    answer: "Most hair masks should be applied to clean, damp hair after shampooing. The slight moisture helps the mask distribute evenly and penetrate better. Towel-dry your hair gently before application, leaving it slightly damp but not dripping wet for optimal results."
