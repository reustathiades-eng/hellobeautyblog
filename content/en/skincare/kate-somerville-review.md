Kate Somerville's ExfoliKate has earned its cult status for good reason. This powerful 2-minute treatment combines physical and chemical exfoliation for immediately smoother, brighter skin.

## What Makes ExfoliKate Special

Unlike single-action exfoliators, ExfoliKate delivers a triple-threat approach: fruit enzymes dissolve dead skin, acids unclog pores, and silica beads polish the surface. The result? Instant gratification.

### The Sensation

Let's address the elephant in the room—yes, ExfoliKate tingles. The warming sensation starts within seconds of application, intensifying before gradually subsiding. It's not painful, but you definitely know it's working.

## How I Use It

- **Frequency**: 2-3 times per week maximum
- **Duration**: Exactly 2 minutes (I set a timer)
- **Application**: On damp skin, avoiding eye area
- **Follow-up**: Hydrating serum and moisturizer

## Results

The immediate results are undeniable—skin looks polished, pores appear smaller, and there's an unmistakable glow. With consistent use, I've noticed improved texture and fewer breakouts.

## Who Should Try It

ExfoliKate is ideal for those with normal to oily skin dealing with dullness or texture issues. Sensitive skin types should approach with caution due to the potent formula.

## The Verdict

At this price, ExfoliKate earns its place in my skincare rotation. The instant results make it perfect for pre-event prep, while regular use genuinely improves skin quality over time.
