Let me be honest: when I first heard about a serum that costs less than a fancy coffee and promises to transform your skin, I was skeptical. I've spent years in cosmetic science labs, testing formulations that cost hundreds. How could something this cheap actually work?

Then I tried The Ordinary's Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1%. And I had to eat my words.

## What Makes This Formula Special

This isn't just niacinamide dissolved in water. The 10% concentration is the sweet spot that research supports for visible results without irritation. Most luxury serums use 2-5% and charge twenty times more. The Ordinary went straight to the clinical dose.

The addition of Zinc PCA is what makes this formula clever. While niacinamide works on the cellular level to regulate sebum production, zinc addresses it from another angle by balancing oil at the skin's surface. Together, they're a one-two punch against shine.

## Texture and Application

The consistency surprised me. It's not quite water, not quite gel — somewhere in between. It absorbs within seconds without any sticky residue, which is remarkable for a 10% active concentration.

I apply two to three drops on damp skin after cleansing, both morning and night. It layers beautifully under moisturizer and sunscreen. No pilling, no interference with makeup.

## Results After 8 Weeks of Testing

Here's what I observed on my combination skin after consistent twice-daily use. The first two weeks showed subtle improvements in overall skin texture. By week four, my pores around the nose appeared visibly smaller, and midday oiliness was noticeably reduced. The real transformation came around week six, when I realized I hadn't had a single breakout in over a month.

## Who Should Use This

This serum is ideal for oily and combination skin types dealing with enlarged pores, excess sebum, and occasional breakouts. If you have dry or sensitive skin, the 10% concentration might be too intense — consider their Niacinamide 5% instead.

## The Verdict

In a world of overpriced skincare, The Ordinary's Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1% is proof that effective ingredients don't need luxury packaging. It does exactly what it claims, backed by solid science, at a price that makes skincare accessible to everyone.

Is it the most elegant formulation I've ever used? No. Does it deliver results that rival products costing thirty times more? Absolutely.
