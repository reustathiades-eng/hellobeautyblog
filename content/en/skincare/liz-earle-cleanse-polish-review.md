With over 100 beauty awards to its name, Liz Earle's Cleanse & Polish has become a British skincare institution. But does this cult classic deserve the hype? After years of use, here's my honest assessment.

## The Hot Cloth Method

What sets Cleanse & Polish apart is its application technique. The included muslin cloth, when dampened with warm water, acts as both a removal tool and gentle exfoliator. This traditional method leaves skin incredibly clean without stripping.

### The Formula

The cream has a rich, balmy texture that melts into skin. The scent—a refreshing blend of eucalyptus and rosemary—transforms cleansing into a spa-like experience.

## My Daily Routine

1. Massage a generous amount onto dry skin
2. Add a splash of water to emulsify
3. Remove with the warm muslin cloth in upward motions
4. Rinse cloth and repeat for a double cleanse

## What I Love

- **Effectiveness**: Removes even waterproof mascara
- **Gentleness**: Never irritates my sensitive skin
- **Value**: A pump bottle lasts 3-4 months
- **Versatility**: Works for all skin types

## The Verdict

Liz Earle Cleanse & Polish has earned its legendary status. The combination of effective natural ingredients, the therapeutic hot cloth ritual, and genuinely excellent results makes this a skincare essential. It's the one product I consistently repurchase year after year.
