Clinique has been a trusted name in skincare for over five decades, pioneering the philosophy that great skin can be created through simple, effective, and allergy-tested products. Founded in 1968 by dermatologist Dr. Norman Orentreich in collaboration with Estée Lauder, Clinique revolutionized the beauty industry by introducing the first-ever dermatologist-developed cosmetics line. What sets Clinique apart is their unwavering commitment to safety and efficacy. Every product undergoes rigorous allergy testing and is formulated to be 100% fragrance-free, ensuring compatibility with even the most sensitive skin types. The brand's iconic Three-Step System - cleanse, exfoliate, moisturize - has become a gold standard in skincare routines worldwide, helping millions achieve healthier, more radiant skin. Clinique's extensive product range caters to diverse skin concerns and types. From their legendary Dramatically Different Moisturizing Lotion that has remained virtually unchanged since 1968, to innovative serums like the Clinique iD system that offers customizable skincare solutions, the brand consistently delivers results-driven products. Their commitment to inclusivity is evident in their vast shade range for foundations and their dedication to creating products suitable for all skin tones and types. The brand's scientific approach to beauty is rooted in dermatological research and customer feedback. Clinique's philosophy of 'simple, safe, effective' guides every product development decision, ensuring that each formula delivers maximum benefits without unnecessary complexity or harsh ingredients. Their makeup products are equally impressive, offering long-wearing, comfortable formulations that complement their skincare expertise. Clinique's sustainability efforts reflect modern consumer values, with initiatives focusing on responsible packaging and ingredient sourcing. The brand continues to evolve while maintaining its core principles, introducing cutting-edge technologies and ingredients backed by scientific research. Whether you're dealing with acne, aging concerns, sensitivity, or simply want to maintain healthy skin, Clinique offers targeted solutions that have been trusted by dermatologists and consumers alike for generations.
