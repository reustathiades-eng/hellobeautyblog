Simple has built its reputation on gentle, no-nonsense skincare. Their Protecting Light Moisturizer combines daily hydration with essential sun protection—at an unbeatable price point.

## Perfect for Sensitive Skin

What sets Simple apart is their commitment to avoiding potential irritants. No perfume, no color, no harsh chemicals—just gentle, effective skincare.

### The Formula

This lightweight moisturizer absorbs quickly without leaving a greasy residue. The SPF 15 provides basic daily protection, while Pro-Vitamin B5 and Vitamin E nourish the skin.

## Who It's For

- **Sensitive skin types** who react to fragranced products
- **Budget-conscious** shoppers seeking quality basics
- **Minimalists** who want simple, effective skincare

## The Verdict

Simple Protecting Moisturizer won't win awards for luxury, but it excels at its job: gentle daily hydration with sun protection. For sensitive skin on a budget, it's a reliable choice.
