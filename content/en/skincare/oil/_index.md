faq:
  - question: "What skin types can benefit from using skincare oils?"
    answer: "All skin types can benefit from skincare oils when the right formula is chosen. Dry skin benefits from heavier oils like argan or marula, oily skin responds well to lightweight oils like jojoba or squalane that regulate sebum production, sensitive skin thrives with gentle oils like chamomile or calendula, and mature skin benefits from anti-aging oils rich in antioxidants and vitamins."
  - question: "When should I apply face oil in my skincare routine?"
    answer: "Face oil should typically be applied as the last step in your evening routine, after cleansing, toning, and serums but before moisturizer if using both. For morning routines, apply oil before sunscreen. Start with 2-3 drops, warm between palms, and gently press into skin. Always apply to slightly damp skin for better absorption."
  - question: "Can I use skincare oils if I have acne-prone skin?"
    answer: "Yes, certain oils can actually help acne-prone skin. Look for non-comedogenic oils like jojoba, tea tree, or rosehip that won't clog pores. These oils can help regulate sebum production, reduce inflammation, and provide antibacterial benefits. Avoid heavy, comedogenic oils like coconut or olive oil which may worsen breakouts."
  - question: "How do I choose between different types of skincare oils?"
    answer: "Choose oils based on your primary skin concerns: jojoba for balance and acne control, argan for anti-aging and hydration, rosehip for brightening and vitamin C benefits, marula for deep moisturization, and tea tree for blemish control. Consider texture preferences too - lighter oils absorb faster while heavier oils provide more intensive treatment."

Our collection includes single-ingredient oils for purists, expertly blended formulations for targeted concerns, and innovative oil-serums that combine the best of both worlds.
