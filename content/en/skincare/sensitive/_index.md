faq:
  - question: "What ingredients should I avoid if I have sensitive skin?"
    answer: "Avoid fragrances, essential oils, alcohol, sulfates, retinoids (unless specifically formulated for sensitive skin), alpha and beta hydroxy acids in high concentrations, and harsh preservatives like parabens. These ingredients can trigger irritation, redness, and inflammation in sensitive skin types."
  - question: "How do I know if a product is suitable for sensitive skin?"
    answer: "Look for products labeled as hypoallergenic, fragrance-free, dermatologist-tested, and non-comedogenic. Check ingredient lists for gentle, minimal formulations. Always perform a patch test on a small area of skin before applying new products to your entire face."
  - question: "What's the best skincare routine for sensitive skin?"
    answer: "Keep it simple: use a gentle, non-foaming cleanser, apply an alcohol-free toner or essence, follow with a fragrance-free moisturizer containing ceramides or hyaluronic acid, and finish with broad-spectrum mineral sunscreen during the day. Introduce new products one at a time."
  - question: "Can sensitive skin use anti-aging products?"
    answer: "Yes, but choose gentle formulations specifically designed for sensitive skin. Look for products with bakuchiol (a gentle retinol alternative), vitamin C in stable forms like magnesium ascorbyl phosphate, and peptides. Start with lower concentrations and gradually increase usage as your skin tolerates."

Sensitive skin requires extra care and attention to maintain its health and comfort. If you experience redness, irritation, stinging, or burning sensations when using skincare products, you likely have sensitive skin that needs specialized care. Our carefully curated collection of sensitive skin products is formulated with gentle, hypoallergenic ingredients that soothe and protect delicate skin without causing adverse reactions. Sensitive skin can be caused by various factors including genetics, environmental stressors, harsh ingredients, over-exfoliation, or underlying skin conditions like rosacea or eczema. The key to managing sensitive skin is choosing products with minimal, clean ingredients that are free from common irritants such as fragrances, alcohol, sulfates, and harsh preservatives. Our sensitive skin range features gentle cleansers that remove impurities without stripping the skin's natural barrier, alcohol-free toners that hydrate and balance, lightweight moisturizers with ceramides and hyaluronic acid for barrier repair, and mineral sunscreens that provide broad-spectrum protection without chemical irritants. Look for products containing calming ingredients like chamomile, centella asiatica, niacinamide, and colloidal oatmeal, which help reduce inflammation and strengthen the skin barrier. When building a sensitive skin routine, introduce new products gradually, perform patch tests, and stick to a simple regimen to minimize the risk of reactions. Our dermatologist-recommended products are specifically chosen for their gentle yet effective formulations that deliver results without compromise.
