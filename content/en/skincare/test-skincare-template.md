This is a test article to verify the new skincare template design. The Ordinary's Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1% has become one of the most talked-about serums in the affordable skincare world, and for good reason.

## First Impressions

The packaging is minimal and clinical, which is very on-brand for The Ordinary. The dropper bottle dispenses the right amount of product, and the lightweight texture feels almost like water on the skin.

## How It Performs

After six weeks of consistent use, the results speak for themselves. Pores appear visibly smaller, oil production is more balanced throughout the day, and those pesky post-acne marks have started to fade. The formula absorbs quickly without any sticky residue.

### Morning Routine

Applied after toner and before moisturizer, it layers beautifully under sunscreen. No pilling, no greasiness.

### Evening Routine

At night, I pair it with a simple moisturizer. The combination keeps my skin calm and balanced by morning.

## The Verdict

At under 10 euros for a full-size bottle, this serum delivers results that rival products ten times its price. If you have oily or combination skin and struggle with enlarged pores or excess sebum, this should be at the top of your list.
