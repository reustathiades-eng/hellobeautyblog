faq:
  - question: "What's the difference between setting powder and finishing powder?"
    answer: "Setting powder is applied immediately after foundation and concealer to lock them in place and prevent creasing, typically using a damp beauty sponge or fluffy brush. Finishing powder is the final step in your makeup routine, applied all over the face to blur imperfections, control shine, and create a smooth, polished finish with light-reflecting properties."
  - question: "Should I use translucent or tinted face powder?"
    answer: "Translucent powder works for all skin tones and provides a natural finish without adding coverage, making it ideal for setting makeup and controlling shine. Tinted powder offers light coverage while setting makeup and should match your skin tone closely, perfect for touch-ups throughout the day or when you want slight color correction."
  - question: "How do I apply face powder without looking cakey?"
    answer: "Use a light hand and build coverage gradually by tapping off excess product from your brush or sponge before applying. Press powder into skin rather than sweeping to avoid disturbing underlying makeup. Focus on areas that tend to get oily like the T-zone, and use a fluffy brush to blend edges for a seamless finish."
  - question: "Can I use face powder on oily skin without clogging pores?"
    answer: "Yes, look for non-comedogenic, oil-free powder formulas specifically designed for oily skin. These powders often contain ingredients like silica or kaolin clay that absorb excess oil without clogging pores. Mineral powders and loose translucent powders are generally better choices than heavy pressed powders for acne-prone skin."
