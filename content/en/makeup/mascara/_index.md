faq:
  - question: "How do I choose the right mascara for my lash type?"
    answer: "Consider your natural lashes and desired outcome. For short lashes, choose lengthening formulas with fiber technology. Thin lashes benefit from volumizing mascaras with thick, creamy formulas. If you have straight lashes, opt for curl-enhancing or waterproof formulas that hold shape longer."
  - question: "What's the difference between waterproof and regular mascara?"
    answer: "Waterproof mascara uses wax-based formulas that resist water, sweat, and tears, making it ideal for special events, workouts, or emotional occasions. Regular mascara is easier to remove but may smudge with moisture. Waterproof formulas typically provide better curl hold but can be more drying to lashes."
  - question: "How often should I replace my mascara?"
    answer: "Replace mascara every 3-6 months for hygiene and performance reasons. Bacteria can grow in the tube over time, and the formula may dry out or change consistency. If you notice clumping, flaking, or an unusual smell, replace immediately regardless of age."
  - question: "Can I layer different mascaras together?"
    answer: "Yes, layering mascaras is a popular technique. Start with a lengthening formula as your base, then add a volumizing mascara for thickness. Allow each coat to dry slightly between applications. Some makeup artists use different mascaras on top and bottom lashes for customized looks."
