    answer: "Most lip glosses last 2-4 hours depending on the formula, eating, drinking, and talking. Long-wearing formulas can last up to 6 hours, while traditional glosses may need reapplication more frequently for optimal shine and color."
  - question: "Can I wear lip gloss over lipstick?"
    answer: "Yes, lip gloss works beautifully over lipstick to add shine and dimension. Apply lipstick first, let it set, then add gloss on top. This technique creates depth and can make colors appear more vibrant while extending wear time."
  - question: "What's the difference between lip gloss and lip oil?"
    answer: "Lip gloss typically provides more shine and color payoff with a slightly thicker consistency, while lip oil focuses primarily on nourishment with a lighter, more natural finish. Glosses offer more dramatic visual impact, while oils prioritize lip care benefits."
