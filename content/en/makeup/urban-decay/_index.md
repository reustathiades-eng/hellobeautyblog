Urban Decay has revolutionized the beauty industry since 1996 with its bold, edgy aesthetic and high-performance formulas. Known for breaking conventional beauty rules, this iconic brand empowers individuals to express their unique style through fearless color and innovative products. From the legendary Naked eyeshadow palettes that redefined neutral makeup to vibrant lip colors and long-lasting foundations, Urban Decay consistently delivers professional-quality cosmetics that perform beautifully from day to night. The brand's commitment to cruelty-free practices and vegan formulations makes it a conscious choice for modern beauty enthusiasts. Urban Decay's eyeshadows are particularly celebrated for their buttery texture, intense pigmentation, and exceptional blendability, making them favorites among makeup artists and beauty lovers worldwide. Their All Nighter Setting Spray has become a cult classic, ensuring makeup stays fresh for up to 16 hours. Whether you're creating a subtle everyday look or experimenting with bold, artistic designs, Urban Decay provides the tools and inspiration to unleash your creativity. The brand's inclusive shade ranges cater to diverse skin tones, while their cutting-edge technology ensures superior color payoff and longevity. From the iconic Vice lipsticks to the innovative Eyeshadow Primer Potion, every Urban Decay product is designed to help you make a statement and embrace your individual beauty.
