    answer: "Matte makeup provides a flat, non-reflective finish that absorbs light rather than reflecting it. Unlike dewy or satin finishes that add shine or luminosity, matte products create a velvety, shine-free appearance that's perfect for oily skin and long-wearing looks."
  - question: "Is matte makeup suitable for dry skin?"
    answer: "While matte makeup is ideal for oily skin, those with dry skin can still use matte products by properly moisturizing beforehand and choosing hydrating matte formulas. Look for matte products with nourishing ingredients and apply a good primer to prevent dryness or flaking."
  - question: "How long does matte makeup typically last?"
