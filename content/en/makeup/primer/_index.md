  - question: "How do I choose the right primer for my skin type?"
    answer: "For oily skin, choose mattifying or oil-controlling primers. Dry skin benefits from hydrating, luminous primers. Sensitive skin should opt for fragrance-free, gentle formulas. For uneven skin tone, try color-correcting primers in green for redness or lavender for dullness."
  - question: "When and how should I apply makeup primer?"
    answer: "Apply primer after your skincare routine but before foundation. Use a small amount and blend evenly across your face, focusing on areas where makeup typically fades first like the T-zone. Allow 1-2 minutes for the primer to set before applying foundation."
