faq:
  - question: "How do I choose the right foundation shade for my skin tone?"
    answer: "Test foundation on your jawline in natural light, not your hand or wrist. The perfect shade should disappear seamlessly into your skin without leaving a visible line. Consider your undertones - cool (pink/blue), warm (yellow/golden), or neutral - and choose accordingly."
  - question: "What's the difference between full, medium, and light coverage foundation?"
    answer: "Light coverage provides a natural, your-skin-but-better look that evens out minor imperfections. Medium coverage conceals most blemishes while maintaining a natural appearance. Full coverage provides maximum concealment for acne, scarring, or dramatic makeup looks."
  - question: "How long should foundation last on my face?"
    answer: "Quality foundation should last 8-12 hours with proper application and setting. Factors affecting longevity include skin type, primer use, setting powder application, and environmental conditions. Oily skin may require touch-ups, while setting spray can extend wear time."
  - question: "Should I use primer before applying foundation?"
    answer: "Yes, primer creates a smooth base that helps foundation apply more evenly and last longer. Choose primers based on your skin concerns - mattifying for oily skin, hydrating for dry skin, or color-correcting for specific discoloration issues."
