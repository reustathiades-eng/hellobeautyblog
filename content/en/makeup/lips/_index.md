faq:
  - question: "How do I choose the right lip color for my skin tone?"
    answer: "For warm undertones, opt for coral, orange-based reds, and warm pinks. Cool undertones suit berry shades, blue-based reds, and rosy pinks. Neutral undertones can wear most colors. Test shades on your lips in natural light for the best match."
  - question: "What's the difference between matte and satin lipstick finishes?"
    answer: "Matte lipsticks provide intense, flat color with no shine and typically last longer but can be drying. Satin finishes offer vibrant color with subtle shine, feel more comfortable on lips, and are easier to reapply throughout the day."
  - question: "How can I make my lipstick last longer?"
    answer: "Start with exfoliated, moisturized lips. Apply lip liner all over lips as a base, then apply lipstick in thin layers, blotting between applications. Set with translucent powder or use long-wear liquid lipstick formulas for maximum longevity."
  - question: "Should I use lip liner with every lip product?"
    answer: "Lip liner isn't always necessary but helps define lip shape, prevents feathering, and extends wear time. Use clear liner for versatility with any shade, or match your liner to your natural lip color for everyday looks."
