    answer: "Consider your undertones: warm undertones pair well with coral, orange-based reds, and warm pinks, while cool undertones complement berry shades, blue-based reds, and rosy pinks. Neutral undertones can wear most shades beautifully."
  - question: "What's the difference between matte and creamy lipstick formulas?"
    answer: "Matte lipsticks provide intense, long-lasting color with a flat finish and typically last longer without touch-ups. Creamy formulas offer hydration and comfort with a slightly glossy finish, making them ideal for everyday wear and dry lips."
  - question: "How can I make my lipstick last longer throughout the day?"
    answer: "Start with exfoliated, moisturized lips, apply a lip liner all over your lips as a base, then apply your lipstick in thin layers. Blot with tissue between layers and consider using a setting powder or lip seal for extended wear."
