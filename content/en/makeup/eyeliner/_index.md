  - question: "What's the difference between liquid, pencil, and gel eyeliners?"
    answer: "Liquid eyeliners provide the most precise lines and intense color payoff, perfect for winged looks. Pencil eyeliners offer easy application and are great for beginners or subtle looks. Gel eyeliners combine the best of both worlds, offering rich pigmentation with smooth application and can be smudged for smoky effects."
  - question: "How do I choose the right eyeliner color for my eye color?"
    answer: "For brown eyes, try purple, green, or navy blue to make them pop. Blue eyes look stunning with copper, bronze, or warm brown liners. Green eyes are enhanced by purple, burgundy, or deep plum shades. Hazel eyes can wear almost any color, but gold and bronze particularly bring out golden flecks."
  - question: "How can I make my eyeliner last longer without smudging?"
    answer: "Start with an eyeshadow primer to create a smooth base. Set pencil liner with a matching eyeshadow on top. Choose waterproof or long-wear formulas for extended wear. Avoid touching or rubbing your eyes, and consider using a setting spray for extra longevity."
